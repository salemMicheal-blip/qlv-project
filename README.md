# QLV — Quantum Levitation Vehicle

## 🚀 About the Project

QLV (Quantum Levitation Vehicle) is a student research and engineering concept exploring how superconductivity, magnetic fields, levitation, sensing, and electric propulsion could be combined in a small-scale experimental vehicle.

## 🧲 The Physics

The project investigates:

- Superconductivity
- Magnetic flux
- Flux pinning
- Magnetic levitation
- Electric propulsion
- Sensors and control systems
- Einstein's theory of relativity

## 🛸 Prototype

The proposed prototype is designed as a small, non-person-carrying experimental platform.

The development stages include:

1. Levitation testing
2. Sensor and control electronics
3. Propulsion testing
4. System integration
5. Controlled testing

## 🎮 Controller

The concept includes an RC-style controller with:

- Dual control sticks
- Small display
- Battery information
- Vehicle speed
- Levitation status
- Temperature information
- Wireless connection status

## 🔬 Research Hypothesis

### Quantum Levitation

If future superconducting materials can operate under less demanding conditions while allowing precise control of magnetic flux, then stable levitation systems may become more practical for engineering applications.

### Relativity

If an object travels at speeds approaching the speed of light, then relativistic effects such as time dilation should become increasingly significant.

## 🌱 Environmental Goal

The project aims to investigate electrically powered systems and efficient energy sources while considering environmental impact.

## ⚠️ Important

This is a student research concept and experimental prototype. It does not claim that anti-gravity or a practical quantum-levitation car currently exists.

## 📌 Project Status

**Current stage:** Website and prototype concept development.

More experiments, research and engineering testing will be required before the concept could become a functional prototype.
