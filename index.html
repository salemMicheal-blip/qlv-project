Quantum Technology

The QLV concept combines several areas of physics and engineering to investigate controlled levitation.

🧲

Magnetic Levitation

Magnetic fields can create forces that oppose gravity. Real-world maglev systems already use electromagnetic principles to reduce contact between vehicles and tracks.

❄️

Superconductivity

Certain materials can exhibit superconductivity when cooled sufficiently. This can produce interesting magnetic effects such as flux pinning.

⚡

Smart Control

Sensors and computer control could monitor position, stability and system conditions in a future experimental prototype.
