01 / Core technology

Four systems. One experiment.

01

Superconductivity

Study materials whose electrical and magnetic behaviour changes below a critical temperature.

02

Magnetic levitation

Investigate magnetic fields and flux pinning as a way to create stable levitation in a small test system.

03

Electric propulsion

Keep forward and backward motion as a separate controlled subsystem rather than assuming levitation creates thrust.

04

Control + sensing

Use a microcontroller, receiver and sensors to monitor the prototype and apply controlled commands.

05

Energy research

Compare practical electrical energy-storage and power options with efficiency and environmental impact in mind.

06

Safety first

Keep experiments small, low-speed and non-person-carrying, with adult supervision and appropriate precautions.

04 / Development roadmap

Build. Measure. Improve.

01

Bench research

Learn the physics, define variables and establish measurable hypotheses before hardware testing.

02

Small levitation rig

Test levitation behaviour with a tiny demonstration platform and record observations.

03

Control system

Develop the receiver, sensors, telemetry and emergency limits.

04

Propulsion + integration

Test movement on a controlled track and combine subsystems after individual tests work.

Research hypothesis: If magnetic-flux control and superconducting systems become easier to operate under practical conditions, then stable levitation could become more useful for future engineering applications.
